Predictive Methodology & Model Transparency

Ethical AI & Clinical Governance: SmartCare AI uses transparent, deterministic queueing models and heuristic heuristics. We explicitly delineate simulated heuristics from production machine learning models to prevent false claims of black-box prediction.

1

Arrival Forecasting (Poisson Process)

Arrivals are modeled using a non-homogeneous Poisson process $\lambda(t)$ modulated by historical time-of-day diurnal patterns and day-of-week multipliers:

P(N(t) = k) = ( λ(t)^k · e^(-λ(t)) ) / k!

In a production enterprise deployment, this is enhanced by an ensemble of XGBoost and Facebook Prophet trained on 3+ years of electronic health records (EHR).

2

Waiting Time Prediction (Little's Law)

Department dwell time and bottleneck detection utilize queueing theory's fundamental Little's Law:

L = λ · W  ⇒  W = L / λ

Where $L$ represents current queue length in the department, $\lambda$ is the service throughput, and $W$ is estimated waiting time.

3

Safe Capacity Matching Matrix

Dynamic bed reallocation evaluates hard clinical constraints to ensure patient safety before recommending transfers:

  • Acuity check: ESI 1/2 never assigned to low-acuity observation
  • Infection isolation constraints (airborne / contact)
  • Telemetry and ventilator hardware availability
  • Mandated nurse-to-patient staffing ratio thresholds
